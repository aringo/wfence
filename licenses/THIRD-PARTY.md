# Third-Party Materials

Wordfence Intelligence records may incorporate material Wordfence does not own —
vendor advisories, external descriptions, and similar content — which remains
subject to the rights and licenses of its respective holders.

Wordfence's license grant cannot convey rights Wordfence does not itself hold.
Consult the licensor's own terms before relying on an individual third-party
record.

The disclosed licensors present in the current snapshot are enumerated in
[../NOTICE.md](../NOTICE.md), which is regenerated from the feed's own
`copyrights` fields on every sync. That file, not this one, is the current list;
this file explains what the list means.

Every record's `copyrights` object is preserved intact in the distributed JSON.
If you derive a dataset from this mirror, carry those fields through — they are
the per-record provenance for exactly this question.
